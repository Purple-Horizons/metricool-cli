## Key Takeaways

1. **Quality over quantity worked.** Two posts generated nearly 1,000 reach and 87 interactions. The engagement rates (12.8% and 4.7%) are well above typical Instagram benchmarks.

2. **The "Mia" voice is a differentiator.** The feed post written as the AI agent outperformed the Reel across every metric. That quirky, in-character angle gave it personality and shareability.

3. **Reels need a stronger hook.** A 66.9% skip rate means two-thirds of viewers bounced before engaging. Consider leading with a bold statement or visual in the first second.

4. **Posting frequency is low.** Two posts in a month limits growth potential. Even adding 1-2 more posts per week could meaningfully expand reach and follower growth.

5. **Stories were unused.** Stories are a low-effort way to stay visible in followers' feeds between posts. Worth incorporating for behind-the-scenes content, polls, or event countdowns.
