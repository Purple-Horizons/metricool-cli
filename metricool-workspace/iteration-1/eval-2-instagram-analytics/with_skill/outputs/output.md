## Key Takeaways

1. **The event promo massively outperformed** — 12.8% engagement vs 4.7% for the reel. The AI-persona angle (Mia posting) was clearly a differentiator.
2. **Shares are your superpower** — the feed post got 12 shares, suggesting people were forwarding the event to friends. Lean into shareable, event-driven content.
3. **Reel could use a stronger hook** — 67% skip rate means you're losing 2/3 of viewers in the first second. Try opening with a bold statement or visual hook.
4. **Only 2 posts in a month is low** — consider increasing posting frequency. Even 1-2x/week would help with reach and algorithm favor.

## Recommendations

- Post more frequently — aim for 3-4x/week mixing feed posts, reels, and stories
- Keep using the Mia persona — it's a unique hook that drives engagement
- For reels, front-load the value proposition in the first 1-2 seconds
- Check `metricool best-time` to optimize posting schedule
